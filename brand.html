The mark is a tile with the Icono Vector Lens: two interlocking curved brackets framing a central focal spark, celebrating the mathematics of vector icon design. Keep clear space around it. Use Clay on Sand or Cream on Clay, never on a photo.

Shape and space

Radius 14px (10px for small controls, 44px for the hero plate). Spacing in multiples of 8px. Borders are 1px at 16% ink, depth comes from borders and tone only, never shadows. Motion: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) for entrances, 200-400ms.
